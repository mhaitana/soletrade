# Soletrade: naming decision and rollout plan

Research date: 24 October 2026, Australia/Melbourne.
Status: owner selected **Soletrade** on 24 October 2026. Product remains under `soletrade.example`.
Implementation: the authorised local app rename is complete and verified. The working tree uses Soletrade / Business and retains Matt Haitana as the parent brand.

## Confirmed direction

The owner selected **Soletrade** as the product name and wants the product to remain under `soletrade.example`. Matt Haitana remains the parent brand. A separate product domain is no longer a selection requirement.

Confirmed identity:

- Product name: **Soletrade**.
- Parent brand: **Matt Haitana**.
- Parent domain: `soletrade.example`.
- Audience benefit: help sole traders and individuals manage their business with ease.

The owner chose the descriptive Soletrade-prefixed approach over the other naming options and authorised applying it across the app. The compact product label is `Business`; positioning copy and product addresses below remain proposals.

## Rationale and proposed positioning

Soletrade identifies what the product helps someone manage and covers invoices, quotes, books, receipts, mileage and future modules. Its generic product label means the descriptor should make its focus on people working for themselves clear.

Proposed positioning, separate from the confirmed name:

> Soletrade
>
> Your business, easier to run.
>
> Invoices, expenses and everyday admin for people working for themselves.

Proposed addresses, subject to deployment planning:

- Product page: `soletrade.example/business`
- Application: `business.soletrade.example`

These are proposed locations, not claims about current routes, DNS or deployments. The web tool could not retrieve the parent homepage, so this report does not assess its current layout or product navigation.

The naming decision does not establish trade mark, business-name or social-handle clearance; those checks remain outstanding before launch.

## Naming brief

The owner's stated goal is to help someone “manage their business easily as a sole trader/individual.”

At the start of this research, the repository described Soletrade Workspace as a unified product for sole traders, freelancers and gig operators. It includes invoices, quotes, lightweight books, receipts, expenses, mileage, earnings and an assistant. Australian pricing and tax features support an Australian-first research scope; the owner has not confirmed expansion markets.

The name should help the owner of a small business feel capable and in control. Prefer familiar sounds, easy spoken spelling and enough breadth to cover future modules. Use a descriptor to explain the product on first contact.

Working assumptions for launch positioning:

- Australian customers form the initial audience, with room to expand.
- Ease and trust matter more than a technical or AI identity.

## Approaches considered

| Approach | Benefit | Tradeoff | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soletrade plus a descriptive product label | Makes the product's purpose and parent relationship clear. | Provides less distinction in search or conversation. | Selected: Soletrade. |

| A distinct product name under the parent domain | Gives the product its own personality within the Soletrade family. | Introduces another name for customers to learn. | Considered; the owner selected the descriptive Soletrade name. |

The owner has confirmed the desired customer benefit, parent domain and product name.

## Final shortlist considered

| Name | Intended meaning | Strength | Main issue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Soletrade** | The Soletrade product for managing your business. | Clear purpose; broad module coverage; familiar spelling. | Generic; needs a sole-trader descriptor. |
| **Soletrade One** | One place to run your own business. | Short product label; fits the connected modules. | Could sound like a plan tier or device; “One” appears in other suite names. |
| **Soletrade Workspace** | Your shared home for business records and tools. | Existing identity; fits the workspace model and product breadth. | Can suggest collaboration or documents before business administration. |

**Soletrade Desk** and **Soletrade Flow** were secondary options. Desk has a friendly admin association but can suggest helpdesk software or office-bound work. Flow suggests ease but gives little indication of the product's purpose.

Limited exact-name web queries for “Soletrade”, “Soletrade One”, “Soletrade Desk” and “Soletrade Workspace” did not reveal a confirmed competing product with those full names. The searches also returned unrelated Soletrade businesses and incidental matches, so they do not establish exclusive use. Check the selected full name and relevant variants before launch.

| Name | Intended association | Main issue |

| Ownhelm | Control over your own business. | Nautical association; can sound like “own elm.” |
| Ownpatch | Looking after your own patch of work. | Can suggest property, gardening or software patches. |

Initial searches did not reveal an obvious competing business-management product for these three names. Ownpatch appeared as a page label in a [game translation project](https://www.thpatch.net/wiki/Portal%3ARu/OwnPatch); spaced “own helm” searches returned software documentation about Helm. These search observations do not establish that the names are unused.

Ordali was another reserve. Its ambiguous pronunciation and spelling made it weaker for a product promising ease.

## Rollout plan

1. **Record the name decision: complete.** Use Soletrade as the selected product identity under Matt Haitana at `soletrade.example`. Retain the other names as research history.
2. **Refine the positioning copy.** The tagline “Your business, easier to run” and the descriptor above are proposals. Optional feedback from five to eight sole traders or gig workers can help assess whether the copy communicates ease and purpose. Choosing the name does not approve a tagline change.
3. **Check the selected name against official records before launch.** Use [IP Australia's TM Checker](https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/trade-marks/search-existing-trade-marks/tm-checker/select) and [Australian Trade Mark Search](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search). Include sound-alikes, spelling variants and the relevant goods and services. IP Australia explains that exact-word searches alone can miss similar marks. Check business-name availability through the [official registration guidance](https://business.gov.au/registrations/register-your-business-name), then check app-store names and social handles as relevant. Extend checks to any confirmed launch markets.
4. **Confirm product locations under the existing domain.** Review current site routes and DNS before selecting the product-page path and application subdomain. Domain ownership, business-name registration and trade marks are separate matters, as [business.gov.au explains](https://business.gov.au/planning/protect-your-brand-idea-or-creation/trade-mark). A separate domain purchase is optional.
5. **Preview the brand in real product contexts.** Review the parent wordmark and product label alongside a dashboard header, signup screen, invoice/PDF, email sender and “Powered by” footer. The current `hAI` assistant name fits the Soletrade family and can remain unless the owner prefers another identity.
6. **Implement and validate the chosen identity: complete in the local working tree.** The app reads Soletrade / Business from the central brand config. Verification passed: 631 tests (217 skipped), type-checking, lint, formatting, branding guard and production build, plus generated public-page metadata checks. After separate owner authorisation, the existing four Stripe products in both live and test mode were renamed and verified; see `docs/research/2026-10-24-stripe-product-renames.md`. External DNS, deployment and email account configuration remain separate. See `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-24-soletrade-rebrand.md` for the execution record.

## Repository rename scope

The project centralises its brand in `src/config/brand.ts`, including separate product name, visual wordmark, compact label, company brand, assistant name and descriptions. The current full logo renders `soletrade`, which fits the confirmed parent brand. Review how to pair it with the chosen product label instead of assuming a replacement wordmark.

The implementation should cover:

- Set the central product name to the chosen `Soletrade` and use `Business` as the proposed compact label. Update product-name references in descriptions; preserve the current tagline until the owner approves replacement positioning copy. Preserve `soletrade` as the parent wordmark and `hAI` as the assistant label unless the owner chooses otherwise.
- Preserve the logo variants: full `soletrade` wordmark, proposed compact `Business` label, and the subtle `h` mark on public documents. Keep merchant identities as invoice/quote issuers, with `Soletrade` as the product fallback; reserve `Matt Haitana` for company/legal contexts.
- Update brand assertions in `src/config/brand.test.ts`, logo tests and the hardcoded name assertion in `src/app/manifest.test.ts`. Add the exact old product phrase `soletrade workspace` to `scripts/check-branding.ts` and update its diagnostics; keep bare `workspace` valid for tenancy.
- Replace hardcoded old-name copy in the admin layout, admin overview and admin user-detail page. Review other occurrences to distinguish active product copy from historical research and technical identifiers.
- Review the shared logo, marketing/auth pages, metadata and manifest, generated icons, email branding and PDF headers against the chosen name.
- Update README/design-system naming and deployment documentation. Plan the product path and application subdomain within `soletrade.example`; check auth redirects, public links, verified email sender settings and billing display names before any rollout.
- Preserve the `soletrade` repository identifier and `workspace` tenancy identifiers. Keep the configured Soletrade company identity and payment statement descriptors unless the owner requests separate changes to those.
- Preserve Stripe `soletrade-*` lookup keys, product/price IDs and subscription references. Update existing Stripe product display names as a separate external task: the setup code reuses matching products without renaming them.
- Run `pnpm branding:check`, type-checking and affected tests; build the app and inspect the named product contexts before release. Read the installed Next.js documentation before implementation, as required by AGENTS.md.

The naming research preceded the owner's implementation request. The authorised local branding pass now updates app code, tests and current documentation. It does not configure DNS, deploy the product, purchase domains or submit registrations.
